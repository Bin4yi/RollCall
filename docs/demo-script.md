## Before recording

- Good light in front of you, phone propped up. Head and upper chest in frame for the heart check-in, your whole body for jumps, about 2.5 m back.
- Stable internet. The SDK needs it throughout, and a weak connection ended every early test.
- Press the landing page's **Follow Amara's morning** to start from fresh demo data. It runs the recordings at 4x speed so the story fits in two minutes.
- Amara's numbers are demo data, and the bundled movement is a generated stick figure. To show real jumps and reps on camera, use your own athlete link in live mode, and open Amara in Judge Mode for the crew part, keeping the banner visible.
- The 9% and 18% figures are from the invented demo story. Don't present them as real results.
