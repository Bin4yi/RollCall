# Demo video: 2 minutes

Screen record with Win+Alt+R so sound is captured. Judge Mode's data is labelled DEMO DATA on screen, so leave the banner in shot. The live check-in at 0:30 must be real and must say LIVE, or say plainly that it's a replay.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | A phone full of messages: "feeling 6/10 I guess", "tired?", "all good!" | "I coach twelve athletes. This is how I find out who should train hard today." |
| 0:15 | The Roll Call board at 6:30 am, sun icons, "10 of 12 checked in, 2 to look at" | "This is Roll Call. No wearables. Everyone's sorted by how far they are from their own usual." |
| 0:30 | Real check-in on a propped phone: framing guide, sunrise, the horizon moving with the pulse | "A private link, 75 still seconds. The sun rises as it reads, and that horizon is my actual pulse waveform, from the SmartSpectra SDK." |
| 0:55 | Two taps: sleep, how you feel. "Checked in" | "Two taps and I'm done." |
| 1:00 | Coach opens Amara: 28-day trends, shaded usual range. "Pulse 10 above usual" | "Amara's off her usual, with intervals planned." |
| 1:10 | Swap to easy, draft appears, edit a word, press Send | "I swap it. AI drafts a note from numbers only, never her name. I edit it, and nothing goes until I press Send." |
| 1:20 | Amara's phone: "Your coach has a note for you", new session | "She has it before her coffee's cold." |
| 1:35 | Simple diagram: SDK signals → personal baseline → coach decides | "Pulse, breathing and HRV against her own two-week usual. It uses 75 seconds because HRV needs 60. Anything it can't read says 'not read today'. It never guesses." |

## Before recording

- Good light in front of you, head and upper chest in frame, phone propped up.
- Stable internet. The SDK needs it throughout, and a weak connection ended every early test.
- Click "Reset the demo" so the board starts at 10 of 12.
- Amara's numbers are demo data. If you check in live as yourself, use your own athlete link, and open Amara from the demo board for the coach part, keeping the banner visible.
